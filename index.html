About Our Hospital

Dedicated to Excellence, Trust & Compassionate Care

Established in 2021 in Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh, Maya's Multispeciality Hospital was built with the vision of delivering comprehensive, super-specialty healthcare services under one roof.

We combine experienced specialist consultations, advanced diagnostic facilities, 24/7 inpatient care, zero-infection modular surgical theatres, and US-FDA approved aesthetic technology for safe, natural, and lasting outcomes.
