Established in 2021 in Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh, Maya's Hospital was created with the vision of providing comprehensive healthcare services under one roof.

The hospital combines specialist consultation, diagnostic services, inpatient care, advanced surgical infrastructure, and modern aesthetic technology to provide compassionate patient care.
